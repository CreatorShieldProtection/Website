What we do

Piracy takes income from creators, and the sites that host it often put visitors at risk. We work on both problems.

Takedown support

We help creators prepare and send removal requests to hosts, search engines and platforms.

Piracy monitoring

Volunteers look for unauthorized copies of creative work and log them for follow-up.

Data safety education

Plain-language guides that show people how pirate sites spread malware and steal personal data.

How a takedown works

You do not need legal training to ask for stolen work to be removed. Here is how we handle each case.

  1. You send us the detailsYour original work and the link where the copy appears.
  2. We check the reportA volunteer confirms the link and finds the right host or platform.
  3. We help you fileWe prepare the request and track it until the content is removed.

Protect yourself from data theft

Pirate sites are a common way to spread malware. These habits keep your information safer.

Avoid pirate and unofficial download sites

Cracked software and free copies of paid content are often bundled with info-stealing malware or fake login prompts.

Use a password manager and unique passwords

If one account leaks, unique passwords stop attackers from reaching your other accounts.

Turn on two-factor authentication

An authenticator app or security key blocks most account takeovers, even when a password is stolen.

Keep your devices updated

Install system and browser updates promptly. They close the holes that malicious sites try to use.

Think before you click or log in

Check the web address, and be wary of pop-ups that ask you to download a player, codec or "update".
